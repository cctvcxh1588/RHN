Our Story

A sea you've never sailed.

There's a race around an island in the South China Sea that most of the world doesn't know about — yet.

Hainan is China's tropical southern tip: 1,900 kilometers of coastline most sailors have never seen. Volcanic reefs. Monsoon winds that have driven maritime trade for 2,000 years. Night passages through ancient fishing grounds. Currents that connect the Pacific to the Indian Ocean. This is where the Maritime Silk Road was born.

Since 2010, the Round Hainan Regatta has been testing offshore sailors on this water. Full circumnavigation. Non-stop. 680 nautical miles from Sanya, around the entire island, and back to where you started. It's been in the World Sailing global racing calendar since 2013.

In 2020, twelve crews battled the worst storm in race history — and completed the full loop without a single port call. Some sailors call it the most demanding weekend of their life. Others come back every year.

You probably haven't heard of it. That's about to change.
Your chapter on this sea starts here.

Why It Matters

More than a race — a legacy in motion.

01 — Sport

Competitive Excellence

Elite offshore racing under IRC and ORC ratings, benchmarking Chinese crews against the world's most seasoned bluewater sailors. Every mile counts, every tack tested.

02 — Heritage

Cultural Celebration

A showcase of Hainan's maritime heritage — from centuries-old fishing traditions to the modern rise of China's tropical yachting capital. Every port of call tells a story.

03 — Community

International Community

Sailors from around the world converge on Sanya each spring — a diverse fleet united by wind, salt and a shared devotion to offshore racing at its most demanding.
