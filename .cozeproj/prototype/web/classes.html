Eligibility

How to Qualify

Every entered boat must satisfy the following three pillars before crossing the start line.

Vessel Requirements

  • Valid measurement certificate (ORC or class-issued)
  • Safety equipment per World Sailing Cat 2 or Cat 3
  • Third-party insurance coverage (min. ¥5M)

Crew Requirements

  • Minimum 4 crew members on board at all times
  • Certified skipper with offshore experience
  • Documented safety-at-sea training (30% of crew)

Documentation

  • Signed and complete official Entry Form
  • Entry fee payment confirmation receipt
  • Race declaration & liability acknowledgement
Entry Process

Register in 4 Steps

01

Submit Entry Form

Download the official entry form, complete all sections, and send it to the Organizing Committee.

02

Payment

Remit the applicable entry fee via bank transfer and share the payment confirmation with the committee.

03

Boat Inspection

Attend on-site measurement and safety equipment check-in at Sanya Serenity Marina.

04

Skipper Briefing

Attend the mandatory pre-race skipper briefing to review course, rules and weather.
