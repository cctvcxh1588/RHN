Common Questions

Quick Answers

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a message using the form above.

01 How do I enter the race?
Download the Notice of Race and entry form from the Documents section, complete all required fields (skipper details, boat specs, IRC/ORC certificate), and email the signed PDF to roundhainanregatta@foxmail.com. Early-bird entry closes 60 days before race start.
02 Who is eligible to compete?
The regatta welcomes IRC, ORC, Multihull and One-Design class yachts with a valid rating certificate. Skippers must hold a recognized offshore skipper qualification, and at least 30% of the crew must have completed a Category-2 offshore safety course.
03 What insurance is required?
Each entered yacht must carry Third-Party Liability insurance with a minimum cover of USD 500,000, valid for the entire race period including delivery legs. A certificate copy must be submitted to the Committee before scrutineering.
04 Where is the race village located?
The Race Village is set up at Sanya Yacht Club on Sanya Bay, hosting registration, scrutineering, skipper briefings, sponsor exhibits and daily prize giving. It's open to the public throughout the event with free entry.
05 Can spectators watch the race?
Yes — the Race Start on Sanya Bay is a spectacular public viewing event. We also stream live tracker data, drone footage and on-boat cameras on our website and social channels. Grandstand seats near the start line can be reserved in advance.
06 How can I volunteer for the event?
We recruit 200+ volunteers each edition for race committee support, hospitality, media, and shore logistics. Applications open 90 days before race start — email volunteer@roundhainanregatta.cn with your CV and preferred role.
