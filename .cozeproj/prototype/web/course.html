Key Waypoints

Navigating the Route

Five signature waypoints define the offshore passage — each with its own currents, character and story on the water.

01
Start · 0 nm

Sanya · 三亚

Race start off Sanya Serenity Marina. Fleet heads southwest, then rounds the island's south cape and turns north along the western shelf.

02
West Passage · ~180 nm

Danzhou · 儋州

Sailing up the western coast past Danzhou — variable pressure zones and tactical route decisions define the leaderboard here.

03
Turning Mark · ~340 nm

Haikou · 海口

The northernmost turn through the Qiongzhou Strait. Crews round Haikou and set course southeast down the island's windward coast.

04
East Coast · ~525 nm

Wanning · 万宁

Passing the surf coast of Wanning — steady NE trade winds accelerate the fleet southward toward the finish.

05
Finish · 680 nm

Sanya Finish · 三亚终点

Fleet returns to Sanya Bay for the finish line — corrected time under ORC/IRC handicap crowns the overall champion.

Inshore Racing

Sanya Coastal Races

In addition to the offshore race, competitors will engage in inshore fleet races within Sanya Bay — a spectator-friendly stage where technique, tactics and starts decide every position.

  • Windward-Leeward Course

    Classic Olympic-format course laid within Sanya Bay, testing crew work and boat handling.

  • Multiple Race Days

    Up to 8 short-course races scheduled across the regatta week, weather permitting.

  • Spectator-Friendly

    Racing action visible from Sanya's beaches and boardwalks — bring the family, cheer the fleet.
