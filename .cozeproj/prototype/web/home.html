The Story

China's Premier
Offshore Sailing Classic

Founded in 2010 in the tropical waters of Sanya, the Round Hainan Regatta has grown from a domestic coastal event into an internationally recognized offshore classic, joining the World Sailing calendar in 2013.

Spanning 680 nautical miles around Hainan Island, the race takes competitors through the South China Sea's most demanding and beautiful waters — from Sanya's tropical shores past Haikou's northern coast and back through open ocean.

Beyond the racing, the regatta celebrates Hainan's rich maritime heritage — blending world-class competition with cultural immersion, dockside festivities, and the warm hospitality of China's southernmost province.

The Journey

From Sanya, Around the Island, Back Home

Starting and finishing in Sanya, the fleet competes across two signature routes: the Full Round (Sanya-Haikou-Sanya) circumnavigating Hainan, and the Half Round (Sanya-Lingshui-Sanya) coastal challenge.

Waypoint 01

Sanya Start

The fleet gathers at Sanya Serenity Marina — the tropical gateway to the South China Sea and starting horn of all classes.

Waypoint 02 · Full Round

Haikou Turn

Full Round boats round Hainan's northern coast at Haikou — the halfway crown and strategic tactical turning point.

Waypoint 03

Sanya Finish

All classes return to Sanya for the finish line — met by cheering crowds, dockside celebrations, and the awards ceremony.
