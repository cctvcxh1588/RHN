The Story

A Sea You've
Never Sailed

There's a race around an island in the South China Sea that most of the world doesn't know about — yet.

Hainan is China's tropical southern tip: 1,900 kilometers of coastline most sailors have never seen. Volcanic reefs. Monsoon winds that have driven maritime trade for 2,000 years. Night passages through ancient fishing grounds. Currents that connect the Pacific to the Indian Ocean. This is where the Maritime Silk Road was born.

Since 2010, the Round Hainan Regatta has been testing offshore sailors on this water. Full circumnavigation. Non-stop. 680 nautical miles from Sanya, around the entire island, and back to where you started. It's been in the World Sailing global racing calendar since 2013. In 2020, twelve crews battled the worst storm in race history — and completed the full loop without a single port call.

You probably haven't heard of it. That's about to change.
Your chapter on this sea starts here.

The Journey

From Sanya, Around the Island, Back Home

Starting and finishing in Sanya, the fleet competes across two signature routes: the Full Round (Sanya-Haikou-Sanya) circumnavigating Hainan, and the Half Round (Sanya-Lingshui-Sanya) coastal challenge.

Waypoint 01

Sanya Start

The fleet gathers at Sanya Serenity Marina — the tropical gateway to the South China Sea and starting horn of all classes.

Waypoint 02 · Full Round

Haikou Turn

Full Round boats round Hainan's northern coast at Haikou — the halfway crown and strategic tactical turning point.

Waypoint 03

Sanya Finish

All classes return to Sanya for the finish line — met by cheering crowds, dockside celebrations, and the awards ceremony.

The Destination

About Hainan —
China's Tropical Island

More than a race venue — Hainan is a Free Trade Port, a tropical paradise, and one of Asia's most welcoming destinations. Here's what awaits you beyond the finish line.

Policy

Free Trade Port & Visa-Free Entry

Hainan is China's largest Free Trade Port. Passport holders from 59 countries enjoy 30-day visa-free entry. Duty-free shopping caps at ¥100,000 per person per year.

  • 59 countries · 30-day stay
  • Sea & air ports open
  • Duty-free shopping
Culture

Culture & Heritage

A tropical melting pot of Li and Miao ethnic traditions, over a millennium of Maritime Silk Road history, and living folk arts from brocade weaving to bamboo dances.

  • Li & Miao villages
  • Silk Road relics
  • 2,000-year fishing heritage
Vacation

Beaches & Resort Escapes

Tropical monsoon climate with 1,900 km of coastline. World-class resorts line Yalong Bay, Haitang Bay, and Dadonghai — a Chinese Hawaii on the South China Sea.

  • Year-round 20–28°C
  • Wuzhizhou Island
  • Yalong Bay resort strip
Cuisine

Tropical Cuisine

Home of the "Four Famous Dishes" — Wenchang Chicken, Jiaji Duck, Dongshan Lamb, Hele Crab. Plus fresh reef-caught seafood, coconut chicken hotpot, and tropical fruits year-round.

  • Four Famous Dishes
  • Coconut chicken hotpot
  • Reef seafood & tropical fruit
