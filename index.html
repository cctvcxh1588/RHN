The Race

About the Race

Founded in 2011, the Hainan Round Island International Sailing Race has grown into one of China's most prestigious offshore sailing events, attracting elite crews from around the world.

Spanning over 1,000 nautical miles around the tropical paradise of Hainan Island, this epic circumnavigation race challenges sailors with diverse conditions — from the bustling shipping lanes of the Qiongzhou Strait to the open-ocean swells of the South China Sea.

Now in its 15th edition, the race has become a cornerstone of the Asian sailing calendar, earning recognition from World Sailing and drawing competitors from more than 20 countries. It showcases Hainan's vision as a global maritime sports hub and a gateway for international sailing in the Asia-Pacific region.

Coastal Sprint

Event Schedule

Registration & Inspection

Yacht inspection, crew registration, safety equipment checks, and welcome reception at Haikou Marina.

Opening Ceremony

Grand opening ceremony with dignitaries, team parades, and the official race briefing. Skipper's meeting and weather routing seminar.

Leg 1 — Haikou to Wenchang

Coastal sprint through the Qiongzhou Strait. Tactical racing through tidal gates and around the northern headlands.

Nov 3

Rest Day & Shore Activities

Wenchang stopover with cultural excursions, sailing clinics for youth, and media events.

Nov 4

Leg 2 — Wenchang to Sanya

Open-water passage along the eastern coast. Trade wind reaching and tropical navigation challenges.

Nov 5

Leg 3 — Sanya to Dongfang

The queen stage. Southern passage around the island's tip with deep-ocean conditions and overnight racing.

Nov 6

Leg 4 — Dongfang to Haikou

Final leg along the western coast. Tactical upwind racing to the finish line in Haikou.

Nov 7

Awards Ceremony & Gala

Grand prize-giving ceremony, champion celebrations, closing gala dinner, and handover to the next edition.

Join the Fleet

Participation

IRC Racing Class

Premier

Compete under the International Rating Certificate (IRC) rule. Open to keelboats 30ft and above with valid IRC certificates.

  • Valid IRC certificate required
  • Minimum LOA: 30 feet (9.1m)
  • Full ISAF safety equipment compliance
  • Minimum crew of 4, maximum per class rules

ORC Racing Class

Open

Race under the ORCi rule for internationally rated yachts. Welcoming a wide range of performance cruisers and racers.

  • Valid ORCi certificate required
  • Minimum LOA: 28 feet (8.5m)
  • Safety equipment per ORC regulations
  • Minimum crew of 4

Unified Class

Amateur

For one-design fleets and amateur teams. A level-playing-field class focused on teamwork and sportsmanship.

  • One-design or handicap-based scoring
  • Open to all keelboats 26ft+
  • Basic safety equipment required
  • Minimum crew of 3
